.venv/

Contains your virtual environment. Keep it out of Git.

data/

raw/: Put your downloaded dataset here.

processed/: Save cleaned data here, after removing NaNs, formatting dates, etc.

notebooks/

Optional: exploratory analysis in Jupyter Notebook. Good for plots and experiments.

src/

data_loader.py: Load CSV or Yahoo Finance data.

features.py: Generate features like moving averages, lagged prices, etc.

model.py: Train ML models (Random Forest, SVM, Neural Networks).

visualize.py: Plot trends, model predictions, and errors.

main.py

The main script that runs everything: load data → preprocess → train model → evaluate → visualize.

     - Real_Rate & SP500: -0.27 - Stocks perform worse when real rates are high

   Weak Correlations:

     - TNX (10-year Treasury) shows weak correlation with most variables except Real_Rate (0.33), suggesting it

   Key Insight: The data shows commodities (gold), stocks, and inflation all move together, while real interest
   rates move in the opposite direction - a classic pattern where rising inflation and asset prices correspond to
   lower real yields.
